## Dusk generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original cinematic Japanese-animation-inspired illustration for the FULL WIDTH hero of a premium personal developer portfolio. Create a wide 16:9 image, ideally 2048x1152. This is finished background art, NOT a website mockup.

Floor-to-ceiling windows overlook a distant delicate city skyline, a few warm illuminated windows and sunset clouds. The sky flows from luminous peach and pale coral at the horizon to soft lavender and rich midnight blue. Subtle star points in the upper sky, refined atmospheric perspective and beautifully painted cloud detail. A warm amber desk lamp creates a small intimate golden pool of light on a wooden work desk in the RIGHT third, with a slim open laptop, a notebook, and a ceramic cup.
Character: one ORIGINAL anime-style young adult woman, about 25, quiet thoughtful software researcher/creator, small in the composition, placed in the RIGHT quarter around x=80%, not the main subject. Dark blue-black shoulder-length hair, simple cream cardigan over a dark top, understated round glasses. Seen in side / three-quarter profile at her desk, looking thoughtfully toward the window, natural restrained anatomy, no fanservice. Face carefully drawn, calm and kind; warm edge light on hair and shoulders, muted violet shadows. The figure should occupy only 20-25 percent of image width.
Composition: cinematic very wide environmental shot. LEFT 45 percent must be dark, calm, spacious negative space (deep-blue interior shadows and quiet glass with subtle reflected sky) so white heading text will be overlaid there in code. No foreground objects or character on the left. The window view and character compose a compelling focal area at CENTER-RIGHT. Architectural framing, warm light and cool sky create depth. Bottom fades naturally into ink blue shadows; no obvious solid gradient graphic.
Style: high-end hand-painted anime film background, exquisite light, fine line work in the character, delicate painterly clouds, soft filmic bloom, subtle depth of field, emotionally warm, healing, elegant and believable. Rich but restrained blue-violet, warm golden lamp light, pale peach sky and luminous whites. Art direction appropriate to an adult developer's sophisticated portfolio, NOT a game banner.
Avoid: ANY text, letters, watermarks, logos, user interface, sci-fi holograms, weapons, school uniform, exaggerated eyes, chibi, plastic 3D render, excessive neon, saturated cyberpunk, clutter, sparkles everywhere, dramatic action, collage, image borders.

## Night edit prompt

Create a matching midnight version of this exact image for a developer portfolio's atmosphere switch.
Change ONLY the time of day, sky, and illumination: the sunset is now a quiet blue-violet night, the distant city lights twinkle softly, a delicate small crescent moon high in the window sky, sparse fine stars, gentle cobalt and lavender cloud shapes. Remove the orange sun and bright sunset band. Keep the amber desk lamp and warm illumination on the character, laptop, wooden desk, and ceramic cup, so the room still feels safe and warm. Slight moonlight edges on windows, no overexposed moon and no exaggerated galaxy.
Preserve EXACTLY the character identity, adult age, pose, facial drawing, clothes, every object, desk placement, room architecture, camera composition, image dimensions, window frames, skyline and mountain silhouettes. Preserve the spacious dark left portion for text. Hand-painted premium Japanese animation film look, cinematic, elegant, quiet.
No text, logos, watermark, extra people, decorative UI, or added objects other than the subtle crescent moon.
